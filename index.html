How it works

Three steps, no IT project.

OnHand reads your product list the way a very fast new hire would — then never forgets it.

STEP 01

Your catalog goes in

Export your products from whatever POS you already use. A setup tool matches your columns — SKU, price, quantity, aisle — and loads them. Most stores are live the same day.

STEP 02

Staff ask in plain words

"Customer wants to hang a 45 lb mirror on drywall." No SKU, no menu, no training. Typed on the floor, or spoken hands-free while carrying something.

STEP 03

It answers from your shelf

The right product for the actual job, with on-hand count and location. If the first choice is out, it offers the best in-stock alternative — from your catalog, not the internet.

Why it's trustworthy

It won't embarrass your staff
with an answer that's wrong.

A tool that invents stock is worse than no tool — your staff repeat it to a customer, and your credibility takes the hit. So OnHand is built to only answer from what's actually on your shelves.
